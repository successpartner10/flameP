# Four Decades of Culinary Mastery

Founded over forty years ago on **Santa Monica Boulevard**, Flame International began with a simple yet ambitious vision: to bring the authentic, royal flavors of ancient Persian banquet kitchens to the vibrant heart of Los Angeles.

![Persian Culinary Tradition](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555939594-58d7cb561ad1?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Timeless Charcoal Techniques

Our cooking techniques remain unchanged from generations of family culinary masters:

1. **Slow Marination**: Meats are marinated for 48 hours in Persian saffron, onions, and delicate citrus marinades.
2. **Mesquite Open Flames**: Skewers are seared over glowing charcoal to seal in natural juices while imparting a signature smokiness.
3. **Golden Tahdig**: Crisp saffron crust formed at the base of copper rice pots, served hot and crackling.

> "We honor our heritage with every dish we serve, welcoming our guests as if into our own home."
