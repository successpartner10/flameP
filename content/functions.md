# Host Your Private Event at Flame International

From milestone birthday celebrations and anniversary banquets to corporate galas and wedding receptions, Flame International offers customizable private rooms and full-venue rentals tailored to your vision.

## Private Spaces & Capacity

* **The Royal Shahrazad Lounge**: Capacity 40–80 Guests. Intimate luxury setting with private bar and sound system.
* **Grand Persian Ballroom**: Capacity 100–250 Guests. Full stage access, dynamic event lighting, dance floor, and state-of-the-art audiovisual setup.
* **Enclosed Heated Patio**: Capacity 30–60 Guests. Al fresco dining ambiance on Santa Monica Blvd.
