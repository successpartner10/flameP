# Welcome to Flame International

For over four decades, **Flame International** has been the premier destination on **Santa Monica Boulevard** for authentic Persian culinary artistry, warm hospitality, and unforgettable live entertainment.

![Flame Dining Atmosphere](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1517248135467-4c7edcad34c4?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

---

## The Art of the Open Flame

Every skewer at Flame is hand-crafted daily by our master grill chefs using prime cuts of beef tenderloin, lamb, and all-natural poultry marinated in saffron, cold-pressed olive oil, and aged Persian spices.

* **Charcoal-Grilled Kababs**: Twin skewers of *Koobideh*, tender *Filet Mignon Barg*, and golden *Saffron Joojeh*.
* **Fragrant Saffron Rice**: Traditional basmati steamed with saffron butter and crisp golden *Tahdig*.
* **Slow-Simmered Khoresht**: Iconic *Ghormeh Sabzi* with fresh herbs and *Khoresht Fesenjan* with pomegranate molasses.

> "True Persian dining is an expression of deep warmth, generosity, and celebration. We invite you to be our guest."

## Live Stage & Cabaret Nights

Every Friday and Saturday evening, our main dining room transforms into West LA's premier Persian concert venue featuring world-class vocalists, live orchestras, and vibrant stage rhythms.
